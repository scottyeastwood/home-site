Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dose of Courage Coaching ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit scottyeastwood.com and related pages (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a call or submit an inquiry, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Usage information. We use website analytics to understand general traffic patterns, such as pages viewed, approximate region, and how visitors find and move through the Site. Some of this measurement uses cookies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule and deliver coaching calls.
  • To send you information you requested, including emails you opted in to receive.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Scheduling and CRM. Our booking and contact forms are powered by a third-party platform (GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), which processes the details you submit.
  • Hosting and analytics. The Site is hosted by Cloudflare and measured using Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics, which help us understand how the Site is used.
  • Embedded video. Some pages embed videos from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. When you play a video, YouTube may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

The Site uses a limited number of cookies, including cookies set by Google Analytics to measure how the Site is used. Third-party embeds, such as a YouTube video you choose to play, may also set their own cookies. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Data retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when we post the updated version, with a new "last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Contact us at hello@scottyeastwood.com.
