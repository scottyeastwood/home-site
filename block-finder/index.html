What's really stopping you?

Direct answer

If you're already driven and producing results but still stuck at the same level, it's almost never effort. It's one invisible block quietly capping what you'll let yourself do, usually fear, a lack of clarity, a priority problem, weak follow-through, or a limiting belief. Name the keystone block and the rest get easier to move.

Most high-capacity people assume they need to work harder. They don't. They need to find the one thing holding the others in place and pull it. The Block Finder walks you through a two-minute diagnostic and names your keystone block, then gives you a specific first move to clear it, drawn from the same Freedom Framework I use with private clients.

The 5 blocks that keep driven people stuck

  • Fear, of failing, and of trying and being seen fail.
  • Clarity, no exciting, measurable target pulling you forward.
  • Priority, doing the urgent and comfortable over the one thing that matters.
  • Discipline, it's clear, you just don't do it consistently.
  • Limiting belief, a quiet story ("I don't deserve it," "it'll cost me," "I'm not the type") running the show.
The science behind the five blocks

These five map onto what decades of behavioral research already found. Piers Steel's work on procrastination ties stalled action to low confidence (fear), unclear value (no target), and distractibility (discipline). BJ Fogg's Stanford behavior model shows behavior fails on motivation, ability, or a missing prompt. Carol Dweck's research on mindset shows a fixed, limiting belief makes people avoid the very challenges that would grow them. Gay Hendricks' "upper limit problem" describes how a hidden belief that success will cost us keeps us self-sabotaging right at the edge of a breakthrough. Different language, same handful of blocks.
