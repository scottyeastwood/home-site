You've worked hard to build what you have. But if growing your business means working longer hours, missing more moments at home, and constantly putting the rest of your life on hold, something needs to change.

In plain terms

A Christian business coach helps you grow your business while keeping God, your family, and your integrity first. In practice that means finding the few actions that actually move the needle, protecting your time, clearing what keeps you from following through, and holding you accountable, all from someone who shares your faith. The coaching is 1:1 and fully online, built on a method called the Freedom Framework, and the first call is free.

What actually makes it different

The order that drives every decision

Business growth is not the highest priority. It never was.

Your relationship with God matters most. Your family matters. And the kind of husband, father, and man you become matters. Your business should help you provide for and serve the people you love, not become the thing that keeps you from them.

That's why faith isn't an additional coaching category or something we discuss only when life gets difficult. It's part of the foundation behind the decisions we make, the goals we pursue, how we treat people, and what we're willing to sacrifice to achieve success.

We pursue meaningful growth, but we do it in the right order: God, family, business.

The method

The Freedom Framework

A simple, repeatable system we run together every week. Here's what actually happens in the coaching.

1

Find the 20%

Identify the small number of activities producing the majority of your results. We separate revenue-producing work from busywork, distractions, and tasks that don't actually need your attention.

2

Protect it on the calendar

Turn your priorities into a weekly schedule. We establish when the important work gets done, what needs to be delegated, and where your time needs to go outside the business.

3

Clear what's blocking it

Identify what's keeping you from following through. Sometimes it's fear, uncertainty, resentment, guilt, or a belief about what success requires. We work through the thinking patterns and behaviors that get in the way, then build a plan to move forward.

Faith should influence more than what we believe. It should influence how we work, how we treat people, how we manage our time, and what we prioritize.

Real results

What actually changes

From 30-hour workweeks to fewer than 20 in the first month.

One client reclaimed more than 10 hours from his total workweek in just 3 to 4 weeks of coaching, going from approximately 30 hours per week to fewer than 20. During that same period, his sales increased.

Using the Freedom Framework, we identified the activities that mattered most, restructured how he spent his time, and built a plan around what actually moved his business forward. The result? More sales, fewer working hours, and more time back.

A multi-million dollar acquisition, stalling at every decision.

Every time he got close to a real decision, the deal slowed down. It looked like bad timing. It was fear, dressed up as hesitation. He kept showing up scared instead of waiting to feel ready, closed and funded the deal, and still runs it today.

Two different kinds of proof: getting more done in less time, and moving through the obstacle that was quietly holding everything back.

Frequently asked questions

What are the benefits of hiring a Christian business coach?

The benefit is growth that does not cost you your faith or your family. You get real strategy and accountability from someone who shares your values, an outside view on the blind spots capping your business, help clearing what keeps you from following through, and a plan that points your hours at the few actions that actually move the needle. The result is a bigger business and more time back for the people it's all for.

What is the difference between a Christian business coach and a regular business coach?

The difference is the order of priorities that governs the decisions. Business growth is not the highest priority here. God comes first, then family, then the business, and that order drives the goals we pursue, how we treat people, and what we're willing to sacrifice to grow. Faith is part of the foundation behind the decisions, not an add-on we only talk about when life gets hard.

Is a Christian business coach worth it?

If you want real business growth without letting your faith or family slip, yes. You get strategy and accountability from someone who shares your values, plus help clearing what keeps you stuck. Every engagement is also backed by our satisfaction guarantee, so the risk sits with us, not you. The best way to find out if it fits is a free call.

How much does a Christian business coach cost?

Coaching is a real investment, and the right number depends on what you actually need. Engagements run from one month up to a full year, and rather than a one-size rate table, we map the option that fits your situation and goals on a free call. Every engagement is also backed by our satisfaction guarantee, with the full terms in your coaching agreement.

Is there Christian business coaching for men specifically?

Yes. This is built for driven Christian men, founders, salespeople, and creators, many of them fathers torn between providing and being present. The whole method is aimed at that exact tension: building leverage so the same hour produces more, without sacrificing God, your marriage, or your kids.

How do you integrate biblical principles into daily business operations?

By keeping the order right, God then family then business, and letting that order drive real decisions, not just intentions. In practice that means letting your faith shape how you lead your team, how you serve customers, how you manage your time, and how you pursue growth, so success never comes at the cost of the people it's for.

Is there a Christian business coach near me?

You don't need one locally. This coaching is 100% online, so we work with driven Christian men across the country and beyond from anywhere. Weekly 1:1 video calls with voice and text support between them mean distance is never the thing holding you back. Follow-through is.

Yes, coaching is 100% online, weekly 1:1 video calls with voice and text support between them, so you can work with us from anywhere. We're based in Hawaii and coach clients across the country and beyond.
