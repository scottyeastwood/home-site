Who this is for

This is not generic business coaching with a cross slapped on it. It is built for a specific person carrying a specific tension.

  • Driven Christian men, founders, salespeople, and creators, already capable and already producing results.
  • Fathers torn between providing and being present, who feel the pull of "I need to work more" against "I am missing my family."
  • Small business owners and earlier-stage builders with capital to invest in themselves, who want to start right instead of spinning their wheels.
  • Men who keep starting and stopping, who know they are capable of more, and are tired of "someday" being the plan.

If you are not a person of faith, or you just want tactics with no regard for what they cost the rest of your life, I am not your guy, and that is okay. This is for men who want to win at work without losing at home.

What a Christian business coach actually does

Here is the part most people underestimate. The person guiding you shapes the advice you get, and that matters more than almost anything else about them. A regular business coach handles the business. A Christian business coach handles the business and the life together, with your faith as the first priority, so the strategy never quietly steers you toward things that pull you away from what you believe.

The strategy is just as real. The difference is the order it serves: God first, then family, then the business. Growth that costs you the people it was all for is not a win, and a good coach will not let you call it one.

The method: the Freedom Framework

Every engagement runs on one simple, ownable system. Not a vague "thrive across pillars" pitch, a specific sequence we run every week.

STEP 1

Find the 20%

Reverse-engineer the small number of actions actually driving your results, and cut the busywork that only feels productive.

STEP 2

Protect it on the calendar

If it is not scheduled, it does not happen. We build the week around the specific number that matters, then protect that block completely.

STEP 3

Clear what is blocking it

When action stalls, the block is almost never laziness. We go underneath it, name the fear, resentment, or guilt, work through it, and re-commit to the action.

That third step is what most business coaching skips entirely, because it is uncomfortable and it is not on a spreadsheet. Courage is not a feeling you wait for. It is what you get on the other side of doing it scared, and I walk you through that part.

How biblical principles show up in the actual business

This is not a devotional with a spreadsheet attached. It is letting your faith drive real decisions, not just intentions.

Keeping the order right, God then family then business, is what sets your calendar before work can claim it. Integrity shapes how you sell and how you serve, so the growth is something you can be proud of. And faith without works is dead: you can believe something one hundred percent, but without action it is empty, so we turn belief into scheduled, accountable moves. Prayer, Sabbath rest, and honest accountability are not separate from the business. They are what keep it from quietly costing you everything it was supposed to provide for.
