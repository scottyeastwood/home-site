What is a time audit?

Direct answer

A time audit is a quick assessment of where your working hours actually go, so you can see which activities drain your time without driving results and reclaim those hours. This one takes about two minutes and estimates how many hours a week you could get back.

Most entrepreneurs are not short on effort. They are short on leverage. The hours leak out through unclear priorities, a day that starts without a plan, constant distraction, doing work someone else could do, and putting off the one task that actually moves the business. You feel busy all day and still finish behind. This audit shows you exactly where your hours are going and hands you the first fix for each leak.

How this time audit works

You answer 10 short questions about how you actually run your week. The audit scores each answer, estimates the hours you are leaking, and ranks your top 3 leaks. Then it gives you the first move to plug each one, drawn from the same Freedom Framework I use with private clients: get clear on your 20%, protect it on the calendar, and clear the fear or guilt underneath the stalls.
