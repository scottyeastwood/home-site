How to Make Time for God When You Own a Business

Direct answer

To make time for God when you own a business, give Him the first part of your day before the business gets a vote, even when you are slammed. Begin with prayer and time in the scriptures, because He is meant to be the priority, not the leftover. Succeeding in business while your faith slips is not a trade worth making, and you will usually find the day goes better, not worse, when God comes first.

Give God the first part of the day, not the leftovers

The reason most business owners struggle to make time for God is that they try to fit Him into what is left after the business has taken its cut, and there is never anything left. Flip it. Give God the first part of your day, before the emails, before the to-do list, before the business gets a vote. Pray as you rise. Feast on the words of God in the scriptures. He is the priority, so He gets the first fruits of your time and attention, not the scraps. If you wait until the business is handled to get to Him, that moment never actually comes.

Succeeding in business while failing in faith is not worth it

It helps to be honest about the stakes. You can win in business and still lose if your faith and your discipleship slip in the process, and that is not a trade worth making at any price. Keeping God first is not a nice-to-have that competes with your work. It is the thing that keeps the whole rest of your life pointed in the right direction.

Put it on the calendar and protect it

Do not leave time with God to willpower and good intentions. Schedule it like any other block you would never cancel, and protect it the same way, phone away, no distractions. Treat your time with God with the same discipline you would give a meeting you truly cannot miss, because it is more important than any of them. Frequently asked questions

How do I make time for God when I'm busy running a business?

Give God the first part of your day before the business gets a vote, even if it is short. Begin with prayer and time in the scriptures, because He is meant to be the priority, not the leftover. If you wait until the business is handled, that time never comes, so put Him first and schedule it like any other block you would never cancel.

Why should I put God first when my business needs me?

Because succeeding in business while your faith and discipleship slip is not a trade worth making. Putting God first is not time taken from your work; it sets your mind and your priorities for the whole day. When He is first, everything downstream tends to fall into better order.

How much time with God is enough for a busy entrepreneur?

There is no magic number, and a short, consistent, undistracted time beats a long one you never actually do. Prayer as you rise and again at night, plus real time in the scriptures, done daily, matters more than the length of any single session. Consistency is the win.
