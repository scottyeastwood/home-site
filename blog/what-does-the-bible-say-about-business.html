What Does the Bible Say About Business?

Direct answer

The Bible frames business as a stewardship. God gives you talents and expects you to magnify them, using your work to build His kingdom and to genuinely serve your fellow man. And the highest form of service is not handing out freebies, it is helping people grow into who they are capable of becoming. Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day; teach him to fish and you change his life.

Business is a stewardship

Scripture is clear that what you have was given to you to grow, not to bury. You are meant to magnify your talents, your gifts, your skills, your time, and your resources, and to point all of it at building God's kingdom and serving the people around you. For an entrepreneur, your business is one of the main ways you do that. It is not separate from your faith. It is one of the arenas where you live it out.

The best service is not a freebie

Here is where a lot of well-meaning people get it backwards. The greatest service you can offer is not giving your product away for free. The best service is showing someone how to grow into the person who can do it for themselves. Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day. Teach a man to fish and you feed him for a lifetime. The people who showed me how to become who I was meant to be gave me something far more valuable than if they had just handed me the result. Building something that truly helps people grow is holy work.

When to give freely

That does not mean you never give. There absolutely are times to give freely, especially when you are prompted by the Holy Ghost and people are struggling to meet their temporal, basic needs. Caring for people in real need is part of what we are here for. Just make sure that even your giving is the kind of help that is true and lasting, not a quick fix that leaves someone exactly where they were.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Bible say about business?

The Bible frames work and business as a stewardship. God gives you talents and expects you to magnify them, using your business to build His kingdom and to genuinely serve your fellow man. Success is not the point on its own; what you do with what you have been given is.

What does it mean to steward your talents as a Christian entrepreneur?

It means taking the gifts, skills, time, and resources God gave you and growing them rather than burying them, then pointing them at building His kingdom and serving others. For an entrepreneur, your business is one of the main ways you magnify those talents.

What is the greatest way to serve people through business?

Not by handing out freebies. The greatest service is helping someone grow into the person who can do it themselves. Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day; teach him to fish and you change his life. There are times to give freely, especially when the Holy Ghost prompts it and people are struggling for their basic needs, but the deepest help is the kind that lasts.
