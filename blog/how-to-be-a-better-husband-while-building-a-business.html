How to Be a Better Husband While Building a Business

Direct answer

To be a better husband while building a business, put your wife on your calendar the way you put your business there. You already keep your business appointments with diligence; if you are not scheduling real one-on-one time with her, she feels secondary. Set weekly time with her, ideally daily, protect it like a client meeting, and be willing to keep adjusting until you get it right.

You keep every business appointment. Are you keeping any with her?

Here is something I had to catch in myself. I was setting appointments with my business every single day and treating each one with diligence, showing up on time, prepared, all in. Then I noticed I was not setting appointments or dates with my wife. You can imagine how that makes a woman feel, like she is on the back burner, like she is secondary to the priorities I claimed she came before. Your calendar tells the truth about what actually comes first, no matter what you say.

Put her on the calendar like you mean it

So set weekly time with your spouse. I try to set daily appointments where I know she and I can have quality one-on-one time together. Treat it with the same seriousness you give the business, because it matters more than the business. Protect it the same way you protect a meeting you would never cancel. And take every chance to connect in between, not just the scheduled ones.

Or bring her into what you are building

Another way to get the time is to bring her into the business itself, so you are spending time together in unity and love while you build. She is your partner. Building something together can be some of the best time you spend, and it turns the business from a thing that pulls you apart into a thing you do side by side.

It is hard, and that is exactly the point

None of this is always easy, and that is where men are made into better men. Being willing to work through the challenge, to rise up, to learn the lessons and adjust until you figure it out, that is the work. It is true of marriage, it is true of raising kids, and it is true of following Jesus Christ. Keep trying, and commit to figuring it out.

Frequently asked questions

How do I be a better husband while running a business?

Put your wife on the calendar the way you put your business there. You already keep business appointments with diligence, so schedule real one-on-one time with her, weekly at a minimum and daily if you can, and protect it like a client meeting. If time is tight, bring her into the business so you are building together. Then keep adjusting until it works.

Why does my wife feel like she comes second to my business?

Because your calendar tells the truth. If you set and keep business appointments every day but never set dedicated time with her, she feels secondary, whether you mean it that way or not. The fix is to treat time with her with the same seriousness and diligence you give a business commitment.

How do entrepreneurs make time for their marriage?

They schedule it on purpose and protect it, they take every small chance to connect, and many bring their spouse into the business so they spend time together in unity. It is not always easy, but working through that challenge is part of how a man grows into a better husband.
