How Many Hours Should an Entrepreneur Work?

Direct answer

There is no magic number of hours an entrepreneur should work. It depends on your season, and some seasons demand late nights and early mornings. The better question is: are your relationships with God and your family slipping? If they are, it is too many. Decide how many hours you actually want to work so you have real time for what matters, then build the leverage to make that number possible.

The number is not the real question

This one is not cut and dry, so be careful with anyone who gives you a clean answer. It depends on the phase you are in. There are seasons of sacrifice where you have to devote a lot, where late nights and early mornings are just part of it. That is real. But the number of hours was never the right thing to optimize for.

The real test: are God and your family slipping?

No matter the season, keep your faith and your family first. Make sure they have their time before you fill in the cracks with the business, not the other way around. So the question is not really "how many hours am I working." The question is: how is your relationship with your spouse, and how is your relationship with Jesus Christ? If either one is slipping, you have probably pointed too many hours at work and not enough at the things that matter most.

Decide the number you actually want, then build toward it

Ultimately it comes down to this: how many hours do you want to work, so that you have more time for God, your family, fun, and adventure? Pick that. Then find ways to make it achievable and work toward it from wherever your starting point is today. The goal is not to grind for the sake of grinding. It is to build the kind of leverage that lets the same effort produce more, so the hours can come home.

A real example

I had one client go from around 30-hour weeks to under 20-hour weeks while making more money. Nothing magic. We focused his time on the highest-leverage actions, used real time-management tools, ran a time audit to find exactly where his hours were leaking, and added the coaching accountability to hold it. Fewer hours, more results, more time for his life. That is what is possible when you use your time wisely.

Check your two most important relationships. How is your relationship with your spouse, and how is your relationship with Jesus Christ?
